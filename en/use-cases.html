Use cases

As many uses as there are screens.

The 36 tiles combine freely. Here are ten layouts to spark ideas; yours will probably be the eleventh.

01 — In the hallway

The morning departure

Before you grab the keys, one look is enough: traffic, the real driving time compared with the usual one plus a suggested departure time, and the day's weather.

Tiles: Traffic map · Commute time · Weather

The breakfast press review

Your RSS feeds scroll by while the coffee brews. A headline catches your eye? A tap opens the article in reader mode, without leaving the board. Markets sit in a corner.

Tiles: RSS feed · Stocks & indices · Quote of the day

Mail and calendar without booting the computer

The latest messages from your IMAP mailbox, readable on tap and strictly read-only, next to the family's merged calendars and a pad for things not to forget.

Tiles: Calendar · Mailbox · Notes · Clock
